❓ How to Play ElementSpeller

⏱️
Speed is the objective! The timer starts running the moment the target word is shown. Try to spell the word completely as fast as possible.
🧪
There are NO strikes or strikeouts! If you submit an element that doesn't match the next letters, you receive instant feedback with no strike penalty—just submit another element.
⚛️
Multiple valid spelling lengths: Words often have several different chemical breakdowns. For example:
• NO can be 1 element (Nobelium [No]) or 2 elements (Nitrogen [N] + Oxygen [O])
• BACON can be Barium [Ba] + Carbon [C] + Oxygen [O] + Nitrogen [N], or Boron [B] + Actinium [Ac] + Oxygen [O] + Nitrogen [N]
🔄
Undo & Reset anytime: You can Undo your last element or Reset the entire puzzle at any point to restart the timer and aim for a new personal best!
📅
Calendar Archive: Browse the full monthly calendar of past daily puzzles in the Archive.
